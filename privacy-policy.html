How MyWatchList handles your data

MyWatchList is a movie & TV tracking app built on The Movie Database (TMDB). This page explains, in full, what the app collects, where it's stored, and how the third-party services it relies on fit in.

01 Overview

MyWatchList is an independently developed app for tracking movies and TV shows, available on Android, iOS, macOS, Windows, Linux, and the web. It has no backend server of its own — every piece of movie, TV, and person data comes directly from TMDB's public API, and everything you track is stored either in your TMDB account or locally on your own device.

There is no analytics SDK, no ad network, and no third-party tracker built into this app. What follows is a complete account of the data that does move through it.

02 What we collect

Your TMDB account

Signing in uses TMDB's own authentication flow. MyWatchList never sees or stores your TMDB password — it receives a session token from TMDB directly, which it uses to read and write your favorites, watchlist, and custom lists on your behalf. Revoking that session from your TMDB account settings disconnects the app immediately.

Data stored only on your device

  • A local cache of your favorites, watchlist, and custom lists, mirrored from TMDB so the app works offline and loads instantly.
  • Detail pages you've opened — movie, TV, season, and person data — cached locally to avoid re-fetching on every visit.
  • Notification follow-state: which TV shows, people, and movie collections you've chosen to get notified about, and a small marker of what you've already been notified for (so you don't get the same alert twice).

None of this local data is uploaded anywhere by MyWatchList. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes all of it. It exists only to make the app fast and to work offline — the durable copy of your favorites and watchlist lives in your TMDB account, not with us.

03 How it's used

  • Reading and writing your TMDB favorites, watchlist, and lists so they stay in sync with your TMDB account.
  • Powering the notifications you've explicitly opted into (new episodes, new credits for people you follow, new entries in collections you follow) — computed and scheduled entirely on your device.
  • Caching TMDB responses locally so screens you've already visited load instantly and remain available offline.

Nothing here is used for advertising, profiling, or sold or shared with any party beyond what's described in the next section.

05 Storage & retention

On your device: a local SQLite database holds the cached data described above for as long as the app is installed. It is never transmitted to us — we have no server to send it to.

On TMDB: your favorites, watchlist, and custom lists are stored in your TMDB account under TMDB's own retention and deletion policy. Removing an item in the app removes it from your TMDB account too.

06 Notifications

Episode, person, and collection notifications are off by default and require you to opt in per item you follow. All of the checking that triggers a notification happens locally on your device against TMDB's public data — there is no push notification server, and no notification content is routed through us.

07 Children's privacy

MyWatchList requires a TMDB account to use its account-linked features and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

08 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what's collected or how it's used, the "Effective" date at the top of this page will be updated, and — where the change is material — a notice will be added to the app's release notes.
